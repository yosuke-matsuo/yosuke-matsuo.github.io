Choosing a method: MM and DFT

Methods differ in speed and accuracy. Use a fast method (MM) to explore many candidates, then an accurate one (DFT) to examine a few.

Method What it does Main use
MM (molecular mechanics) Represents bonds and angles with spring-like equations (a force field). No electrons. Under a second per structure. Initial structures, conformer searches
Semiempirical QM (e.g. xTB) Simplified quantum mechanics with fitted parameters. Handles electronic structure better than MM. Intermediate screening of many candidates
DFT Computes the electronic structure from the electron density. Tens of minutes to hours per structure. Optimization, energies, frequencies, NMR/ECD

In DFT you choose three things: the functional (approximation of electron interactions, e.g. B3LYP), the basis set (how the electron distribution is represented, e.g. 6-31G(d)) and the solvent model (e.g. SMD).

MM and DFT often rank conformers differently. Keep a generous set of candidates at the MM stage.

The basic sequence

  1. Build the molecule

    Fix stereochemistry, charge and protonation state here.

  2. Search conformers with MM

    Cover rotatable bonds, ring shapes and OH orientations.

  3. Remove duplicates and optimize with DFT (Opt Freq)

    Zero imaginary frequencies means a minimum.

  4. Compare energies and obtain populations

    Compare G values obtained under the same conditions.

  5. Compute properties and average by population

    NMR, ECD, optical rotation, ...

Decide before you start

  • Species, stereochemistry, charge, multiplicity, solvent, temperature, and the experimental data to compare with.
  • A naming rule for structures (e.g. cat_c01_opt). You will end up with dozens of files.
  • The same conditions for every candidate. Values from different conditions cannot be compared.
