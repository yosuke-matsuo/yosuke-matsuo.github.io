Glossary

FilesJob typesMethods and settingsReading output

Files

Term Meaning
.gjf / .com Gaussian input file: a text file with the calculation settings and the molecular coordinates.
.log / .out Gaussian output file. Energies, geometries, frequencies and errors are all written as text. The extension depends on the environment.
.chk (checkpoint) A binary file holding intermediate results. Set with %chk=name; read from another job with %oldchk.
Route line The line in the input starting with #. Specifies the method (functional/basis set) and job type (Opt, Freq, NMR, ...).

Job types

Term Meaning
Opt (geometry optimization) Moves atoms step by step to find a structure where the forces are nearly zero (a minimum or a TS).
Freq (frequency analysis) Computes vibrational frequencies at the optimized structure. All positive: a minimum; exactly one negative (imaginary): a TS. Thermodynamic quantities (G, H) come from here too.
SP (single point) Computes the energy without moving the geometry. Used to refine energies with a larger basis set.
TS (transition-state search) Opt=TS. Searches for the top of a reaction barrier (first-order saddle point). Usually run with CalcFC to compute the initial Hessian.
IRC (intrinsic reaction coordinate) Follows the downhill path from a TS toward reactant and product to confirm which structures the TS connects.
NMR (GIAO) Computes nuclear shielding constants σ. Chemical shifts δ come from the difference with a reference or from a calibration equation.
TD (time-dependent DFT) Computes excited states; used for UV and ECD spectra.

Methods and settings

Term Meaning
Functional The approximation for electron–electron interactions in DFT. Specified by names such as B3LYP, M06-2X, ωB97X-D.
Basis set The set of functions describing the electron distribution: 6-31G(d), 6-311+G(d,p), def2-TZVP, ... Larger means more accurate and slower.
Dispersion correction (D3) Adds weak attractive (dispersion) interactions to DFT. EmpiricalDispersion=GD3BJ. Important for conformations and intermolecular interactions.
Solvent model (PCM, SMD) Approximates the solvent as a continuum. Specified as e.g. SCRF=(SMD,Solvent=Methanol).
Charge and multiplicity Total charge and spin multiplicity (2S+1). Examples: neutral closed-shell 0 1; closed-shell monoanion -1 1; neutral doublet radical 0 2.
SCF The iterative solution of the electronic structure at one geometry. Failure to converge gives Convergence failure.
Hessian / CalcFC Second derivatives of the energy (force constants), used to choose optimization steps. CalcFC computes it exactly at the first cycle.
MM (molecular mechanics) / MMFF A fast method that treats bonds and angles as springs without electrons. Used for conformer searches and initial structures.

Reading output

Term Meaning
Normal termination Shows that the program finished normally.
Error termination Abnormal end. The cause is printed a few lines above.
Stationary point found Shows that the optimization converged; normally printed when all four Converged? items are YES (it can also appear with displacements NO when the forces are very small).
Imaginary frequency A frequency reported as negative; moving along it lowers the energy. A minimum has none; a TS has exactly one.
E and G E (the SCF Done value) is the electronic energy; G (Sum of electronic and thermal Free Energies) is the Gibbs free energy. Conformer populations are usually computed from G (some protocols use E).
Boltzmann distribution Gives conformer populations from energy differences (usually G): pi ∝ exp(−ΔGi/RT). Properties are averaged with these weights.
DP4+ A statistical method that assigns probabilities to candidate structures from the agreement between calculated and experimental NMR.
