Glossary

FilesJob typesMethods and settingsReading output

Files

Term Meaning
.gjf / .com Gaussian input file: a text file with the calculation settings and the molecular coordinates.
.log / .out Gaussian output file. Energies, geometries, frequencies and errors are all written as text. The extension depends on the environment.
.chk (checkpoint) A binary file that stores the results of a job (geometry, wavefunction and so on). Set with %chk=name; read from another job with %oldchk.

Job types

Term Meaning
Opt (geometry optimization) Moves atoms step by step to find a structure where the forces are nearly zero: a minimum by default, or a transition state (TS) with Opt=TS.
Freq (frequency analysis) Computes vibrational frequencies at the optimized structure. The Freq job also gives thermochemical quantities (G, H).
SP (single-point calculation) Computes the energy without moving the geometry. Used to refine energies with a larger basis set.
TS (transition-state search) Opt=TS. Searches for the top of a reaction barrier (first-order saddle point). CalcFC is often used (or ReadFC to read the force constants from an earlier job).
IRC (intrinsic reaction coordinate) Follows the downhill path from a TS toward reactant and product to confirm which structures the TS connects.
NMR (GIAO) Computes nuclear shielding constants σ. Chemical shifts δ are obtained from the difference from a reference compound or from a calibration equation.
TD (excited-state calculation) Computes excited states by time-dependent HF or DFT (the examples in this guide are TD-DFT); used for UV and ECD spectra.

Methods and settings

Term Meaning
Route line The line of the input that starts with #. Specifies the method (functional/basis set) and job type (Opt, Freq, NMR, ...).
Functional The approximation for electron exchange and correlation in DFT. Specified by names such as B3LYP, M06-2X and ωB97X-D (written M062X and wB97XD on the route line).
Basis set The set of functions that represent the molecular orbitals: 6-31G(d), 6-311+G(d,p), def2-TZVP, ... Larger sets generally give higher accuracy and take longer to compute.
Dispersion correction (D3) Adds weak attractive (dispersion) interactions to DFT. EmpiricalDispersion=GD3BJ. Matters for conformer energy differences and intermolecular interactions.
Solvent model (PCM, SMD) Approximates the solvent as a continuum. Specified as e.g. SCRF=(SMD,Solvent=Methanol).
Charge and multiplicity Total charge and spin multiplicity (2S+1). Examples: neutral closed-shell 0 1; closed-shell monoanion -1 1; neutral doublet radical 0 2.
SCF The iterative calculation of the electronic structure at one geometry. If it does not converge, the output shows Convergence failure.
Hessian / CalcFC Second derivatives of the energy (force constants), used to choose optimization steps. CalcFC computes it exactly at the first cycle.
MM (molecular mechanics) / MMFF A fast method that describes bonds and angles with spring-like equations, without treating electrons. Used for conformer searches and initial structures.

Reading output

Term Meaning
Normal termination Shows that the program finished normally.
Error termination The job ended with an error. The cause is printed a few lines above.
Stationary point found Shows that the optimization converged to a stationary point (a structure where the forces are nearly zero: a minimum or a TS); normally printed when all four Converged? items are YES (also when Maximum Force and RMS Force are below 1/100 of their thresholds, whatever the displacements). With Opt Freq the test is repeated in the Freq step; if it is not printed there, the structure is not a stationary point.
Imaginary frequency A frequency reported as a negative number; moving the structure along this mode lowers the energy. A minimum has none; a TS has exactly one.
E and G E (the SCF Done value) is the electronic energy; G (Sum of electronic and thermal Free Energies) is the Gibbs free energy. Conformer populations are usually computed from G.
Boltzmann distribution Gives conformer populations from energy differences (usually G): pi ∝ exp(−ΔGi/RT). Properties are averaged with these weights.
DP4+ A statistical method that assigns probabilities to candidate structures from the agreement between calculated and experimental NMR.
