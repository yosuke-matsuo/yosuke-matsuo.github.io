What to report in papers and SI

Items to reportRecords to keep per property

Items to report

  • Gaussian version, functional, basis set, dispersion correction, solvent model and solvent.
  • Charge/multiplicity, temperature, energy definition (E or G, with or without SP correction).
  • Conformer search method and selection criteria, number of conformers used and their populations.
  • NMR/J calibration equations with the source of the coefficients, and atom assignments.
  • For a TS: the imaginary frequency and the IRC result.
  • Coordinates and energies of all optimized structures, and the number of imaginary frequencies.

Records to keep per property

Also keep the uncorrected values and conversion equations. Keeping concise tables for the paper separate from the traceable calculation record makes checking easier.

  • All properties: energy used for weighting, temperature, conformers used and the share of the population they cover, reasons for exclusions, computational model.
  • ECD/UV: gauge, band shape, width value and definition, units, display range and step, equation sources. If the curves were shifted or scaled, keep the uncorrected curves and a record of each operation.
  • NMR: nuclide, atom assignments, raw shielding, reference standard or direction of the calibration equation (σ to δ or δ to σ), coefficients, source and applicability. State whether you used a published calibration or a fit to the same experimental data.
