IRC errors

What to do when a reaction-path calculation stops, and retracing with LQA.

IRC errors

Corrector does not converge

Maximum number of corrector steps exceded

Cause: The path-correction iterations (corrector) did not converge.

What to do
  1. Check whether an SCF failure or a memory/disk shortage happened first; fix that if so.
  2. If this error occurs with the default algorithm (no option, or IRC=HPC), recompute from the same TS with IRC=LQA: read the TS Freq .chk with %oldchk and keep the direction, MaxPoints and StepSize of the original job (input below).
  3. Reducing StepSize (e.g. 10 → 5) also helps.

IRC point limit reached

Maximum number of steps reached.

Cause: The IRC ran to MaxPoints and stopped normally, but the endpoint may not have reached a minimum yet.

What to do
  1. Look at the endpoint structure. If it is still changing a lot, continue with a larger MaxPoints: either extend from the stopped IRC .chk with IRC=(Restart,MaxPoints=N), or recompute from the TS Freq .chk with a larger MaxPoints (same form as the input below, with IRC=(RCFC,…) and %oldchk).
  2. If it looks like reactant/product, relax the endpoint with Opt Freq and confirm a minimum.

Endpoint Opt step limit

Number of steps exceeded,  NStep= 100

Cause: The optimization from an IRC endpoint stopped at the cycle limit.

What to do

  1. Same remedy as an ordinary Opt (Opt step limit).

  2. Decide which end is reactant or product from the final structure, not from the Forward/Reverse labels.

Example input for retracing with LQA

%oldchk=ts_freq.chk
%chk=ts_irc_fwd_lqa.chk
#p B3LYP/6-31G(d) SCRF=(SMD,Solvent=Methanol)
 IRC=(RCFC,Forward,LQA,MaxPoints=80,StepSize=5) Geom=AllCheck Guess=Read

If Reverse failed, change Forward to Reverse and %chk to ts_irc_rev_lqa.chk. Geom=AllCheck reads the title, geometry, charge and multiplicity from the .chk, so the title, charge/multiplicity line and coordinates are not written.
