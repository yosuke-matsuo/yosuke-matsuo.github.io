J coupling constants: components and scaling

The spin–spin coupling constant J (Hz) is the sum of four terms: Fermi contact (FC), spin–dipole (SD), and paramagnetic/diamagnetic spin–orbit (PSO/DSO). For ¹H–¹H couplings the FC term dominates, so computing only FC and applying a scaling factor (e.g. 0.9155) is common.

  • Scaling factors are tied to the conditions (functional, basis set, FC-only or total).
  • J is sensitive to conformation, so the accuracy of the populations directly affects the result.

References

  • Bally & Rablen, J. Org. Chem. 2011, 76, 4818

Calculation conditions: ¹H–¹H J (Bally–Rablen)

B3LYP/6-31G(d,p)u+1s, FC-only (IOp version)

The convenient form recommended by CHESHIRE. IOp(3/10=1100000) augments the basis on all atoms for FC; atoms=H restricts J to hydrogens. Geometries at gas-phase B3LYP/6-31G(d). Scaling: J = 0.9117 × FC.

Relative time: reference (1)

%chk=j-cheshire.chk
#p RB3LYP/6-31G(d,p) NMR=(FCOnly,ReadAtoms)
   IOp(3/10=1100000)

After the coordinate block: a blank line, then a line atoms=H, then a blank line.

Used in: Shi et al., J. Org. Chem. 2023 · Grauso et al., J. Nat. Prod. 2022, 85, 2468 · Li et al., Org. Lett. 2021, 23, 7134

Source: Bally & Rablen, J. Org. Chem. 2011, 76, 4818 · CHESHIRE: Recommendations

B3LYP/6-31G(d,p)u+1s[H], FC-only (Gen version)

The original recommendation. Only hydrogen gets 8 uncontracted s functions plus 1 p function (via Gen); other elements use 6-31G(d,p). Scaling: J = 0.9155 × FC. About 1/6 the time of the IOp version (Table 5 of the paper).

Relative time: shorter than 1

%chk=bally-fc-example.chk
#p B3LYP/Gen NMR=(FCOnly,ReadAtoms) Integral=NoBasisTransform

Complete input (methane as a format example; replace the coordinates with your molecule):

Scaling and averaging

  • Average the signed FC values over conformers, then multiply by the factor (0.9117 or 0.9155), and take absolute values last if needed.
  • The factors are specific to FC-only calculations with that basis augmentation. Do not use them for ordinary-basis FC, total J or ¹H–¹³C.
  • In the paper's gas-phase test, scaled RMS errors are 0.49 Hz (IOp) and 0.51 Hz (Gen), essentially equal.

Total-J calculations (all four terms)

Computes all four terms (FC, SD, PSO, DSO). In Gaussian, specify NMR=SpinSpin. Systematic errors remain even with all terms, so apply scaling only when a matched, validated factor exists; otherwise report as unscaled.

PBE/pcJ-3, CPCM (example in ORCA)

Used for sugar conformational analysis with all terms explicitly computed, using the J-optimized pcJ basis. In Gaussian, PBE is written PBEPBE and pcJ-3 basis cards for every element are given via Gen.

Relative time: much longer than 1

#p PBEPBE/Gen NMR=SpinSpin SCRF=(CPCM,Solvent=Acetonitrile)
(pcJ-3 cards for all elements after the coordinates)

B97-2/pcJ-1, IEFPCM (reported for natural products)

Other nuclear pairs (e.g. ¹H–¹³C)

The ¹H–¹H factors do not apply. Use the literature level and its correction together.

PBE0/6-311G(d,p), IEFPCM(CHCl₃), total J

Used in CASE-3D for ²JC,H and ³JC,H of strychnine. No scaling; the error against experiment feeds an AIC evaluation.

Relative time: longer than 1

#p PBE1PBE/6-311G(d,p) NMR=SpinSpin
   SCRF=(IEFPCM,Solvent=Chloroform)

Used in: CASE-3D, Angew. Chem. Int. Ed. 2020

ωB97X-D/6-311G(d,p), Mixed, total J

Used to generate IMPRESSION training data; geometries at mPW1PW91/6-311G(d,p). For ¹JC,H, an offset of −10.91 Hz was added (specific to that dataset).

J-DP4

Whereas DP4+ assigns probabilities from chemical shifts alone, J-DP4 also uses experimental ³JH,H, in three modes.

Mode How J is used J calculation needed?
iJ-DP4 Conformers inconsistent with experimental ³JH,H are removed first; DP4 on shifts follows. Not required (J estimates on MMFF structures suffice)
dJ-DP4 Agreement between calculated and experimental J is included in the probability. Required
iJ/dJ-DP4 Both combined. Required

J conditions for dJ-DP4 (original paper)

Gaussian 09, B3LYP/6-31G(d,p), FC only, gas-phase MMFF geometries. Conformers within 5 kcal/mol, Boltzmann-averaged at 298 K on the NMR single-point electronic energies. Scaling: Jscaled = (Jcalc + 0.14)/0.95 (regression on the paper's 69 cases; distinct from the Bally–Rablen factor 0.9117).

#p B3LYP/6-31G(d,p) NMR=(GIAO,FCOnly,ReadAtoms)

(title / charge mult / MMFF coordinates / blank line)
atoms=H

The paper does not give its original route line or IOp; this is constructed from the reported level.

ML-J-DP4

A fast variant: ³JH,H is estimated from dihedral angles with a Karplus-type equation (Haasnoot–de Leeuw–Altona) and HF/STO-3G shieldings are corrected by machine learning. Only the conformer search and shielding calculation are needed, so it is much lighter. Use the official Python implementation.
