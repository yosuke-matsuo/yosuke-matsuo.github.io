HRMS Calc (HRMS Exact Mass Calculator)

A web tool for exact masses in high-resolution mass spectrometry (runs in the browser; MIT License).

  • Calculates m/z from a formula and an ion type, taking the electron mass into account, and shows the error from a measured value (ppm, mDa) and the isotope pattern
  • Finds candidate formulas for a measured m/z within a tolerance and per-element atom-count ranges
  • Calculates many compounds at once and writes HRMS statements for papers and SI
  • Checks pasted HRMS statements from papers and SI for errors in the calculated values (such as the electron mass)
