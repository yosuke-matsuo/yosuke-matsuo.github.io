Kikuchi, K.; Matsuo, Y.*; Saito, Y.; Yamada, K.; Tanaka, T.; Yakushiji, F.* Structure Revision of Isomallotusinin to Mallotusinin by NMR-Based Conformational Analysis J. Nat. Prod. 2026, 89, 64–72. doi:10.1021/acs.jnatprod.5c01064 ｜ ChemRxiv ｜ NAOSITE

Isomallotusinin, reported in 2012 as an anomer of the ellagitannin mallotusinin, was shown to be identical to mallotusinin, correcting a structural misassignment. DFT-assisted analysis of ¹H–¹H coupling constants showed that mallotusinin, which bears a tetrahydroxydibenzofuranoyl (THDBF) group, exists in solution as an equilibrium of 3S1 (skew-boat) and 1C4 (chair) conformers whose ratio depends on the solvent. The work highlights the importance of conformational dynamics in the structure elucidation of conformationally flexible ellagitannins.

Matsuo, Y.*; Ohtsuka, R.; Saito, Y.; Yamada, K.; Tanaka, T.; Yakushiji, F.* Stereochemical revision of brevipetins E and F: ellagitannin dimers with opposite hexahydroxydiphenoyl atropisomerism Org. Biomol. Chem. 2025, 23, 10214–10224. doi:10.1039/d5ob01323b ｜ ChemRxiv ｜ NAOSITE

Brevipetins E and F, ellagitannin dimers isolated from Cleidion brevipetiolatum in 2019, were reported to bear 3,4-(Ra)-hexahydroxydiphenoyl (HHDP) groups on both the glucose and rhamnose units. Re-evaluation of the reported electronic circular dichroism (ECD) data, supported by DFT calculations of NMR chemical shifts and ECD spectra, showed that the two HHDP groups in the molecule have opposite atropisomerism, the rhamnose HHDP group having the (Sa) configuration.

Ohtsuka, R.; Matsuo, Y.*; Saito, Y.; Yamada, K.; Tanaka, T.; Yakushiji, F.* Structural revisions of macrocyclic ellagitannin dimers bridged by two hexahydroxydiphenoyl groups Tetrahedron 2025, 184, 134751. doi:10.1016/j.tet.2025.134751 ｜ ChemRxiv ｜ NAOSITE

Two ellagitannins isolated in 2024 from the wood of sweet chestnut (Castanea sativa) and reported as unprecedented macrocyclic dimers bridged by two HHDP groups were shown to be the known monomers 2,3-O-(Sa)-HHDP-D-glucopyranose and gemin D. The structures were revised by comparing the reported NMR data with those of authentic samples, together with DFT-based NMR chemical shift calculations.

Matsuo, Y.*; Iki, M.; Okubo, C.; Saito, Y.; Tanaka, T. Conformationally flexible ellagitannins: conformational analysis of davidiin and punicafolin by DFT-based ¹H–¹H coupling constant calculations New J. Chem. 2025, 49, 11068–11079. doi:10.1039/d5nj01278c ｜ ChemRxiv ｜ NAOSITE

Ellagitannins adopt various conformations of their glucose core. DFT calculations of ¹H–¹H coupling constants showed that, in solution, davidiin (found in Davidia involucrata and Persicaria capitata) exists as an equilibrium of BO,3 (boat) and 1C4 (chair) conformations, and punicafolin (found in Punica granatum and Mallotus japonicus) as an equilibrium of 3S1 (skew-boat) and 1C4 conformations; both equilibria shift with solvent and temperature. Such flexibility may underlie the biosynthesis of structurally diverse ellagitannins.

Yamashita, T.; Matsuo, Y.*; Saito, Y.; Tanaka, T.* Formation of Dehydrohexahydroxydiphenoyl Esters by Oxidative Coupling of Galloyl Esters in an Aqueous Medium Involved in Ellagitannin Biosynthesis Chem. Asian J. 2021, 16, 1735–1740. doi:10.1002/asia.202100380

Hexahydroxydiphenoyl (HHDP) and dehydrohexahydroxydiphenoyl (DHHDP) groups are the major acyl units of ellagitannins, but how they are biosynthesized in plants was unknown. CuCl2-mediated oxidation of simple galloyl derivatives in water coupled two galloyl groups to give DHHDP-type products, which were reduced to HHDP-type products, whereas HHDP-type products were not oxidized to DHHDP-type products. We therefore proposed that in plants the DHHDP group forms first and the HHDP group is then biosynthesized by subsequent reduction.

Kawazoe, R.; Matsuo, Y.*; Saito, Y.; Tanaka, T.* Stereochemistry of a Cyclic Epicatechin Trimer with C3 Symmetry Produced by Oxidative Coupling Eur. J. Org. Chem. 2021, 777–781. doi:10.1002/ejoc.202001579

Oxidation of (−)-epicatechin with polyphenol oxidase gave a C3-symmetric cyclic trimer linked through A-ring–B-ring C–C bonds, previously obtained with CuCl2 but assigned only a planar structure. Its stereochemistry was determined by 1D and 2D NMR together with theoretical calculations of NMR and ECD data, suggesting that the less strained (aR,aR,aR) isomer forms atroposelectively during cyclization.

Kawazoe, R.; Matsuo, Y.*; Saito, Y.; Tanaka, T.* Computationally Assisted Structural Revision of Flavoalkaloids with a Seven-Membered Ring: Aquiledine, Isoaquiledine, and Cheliensisine J. Nat. Prod. 2020, 83, 3347–3353. doi:10.1021/acs.jnatprod.0c00691

Aquiledine (from Aquilegia ecalcarata) and cheliensisine (from Goniothalamus cheliensis) had been assigned different structures despite virtually identical ¹H and ¹³C NMR data. Their structures, and that of the regioisomer isoaquiledine, were revised on the basis of DFT calculations of NMR data, including DP4+ and J/DP4 analyses, and of specific rotations.

Matsuo, Y.*; Yoshida, A.; Saito, Y.; Tanaka, T.* Structural Revision and Biomimetic Synthesis of Goupiolone B Angew. Chem. Int. Ed. 2017, 56, 11855–11859. doi:10.1002/anie.201706532 ｜ NAOSITE

The structure of goupiolone B, a phenolic compound with significant DNA-damaging activity reported in 2003, was revised on the basis of DFT calculations of ¹³C NMR chemical shifts and biosynthetic considerations. The revised dibenzobicyclo[3.2.2]nonane skeleton implied formation by oxidative coupling of catechol and goupiolone A, which was supported by biomimetic synthesis. During attempts at chiral resolution of synthetic racemic goupiolone B to determine the absolute configuration of the natural product, goupiolone B was found to racemize, and a mechanism involving α-ketol rearrangement is proposed.

Matsuo, Y.*; Oowatashi, R.; Saito, Y.; Tanaka, T.* Nonenzymatic Biomimetic Synthesis of Black Tea Pigment Theaflavins Synlett 2017, 28, 2505–2508. doi:10.1055/s-0036-1588529

Theaflavins are reddish-orange black-tea pigments with a benzotropolone chromophore, formed by oxidative coupling of catechol-type and pyrogallol-type catechins via bicyclo[3.2.1]octane-type intermediates. We developed a new nonenzymatic biomimetic synthesis of theaflavins using the DPPH radical as the oxidant.

Matsuo, Y.*; Okuda, K.; Morikawa, H.; Oowatashi, R.; Saito, Y.; Tanaka, T.* Stereochemistry of the Black Tea Pigments Theacitrins A and C J. Nat. Prod. 2016, 79, 189–195. doi:10.1021/acs.jnatprod.5b00832

Theacitrins A–C are yellow pigments isolated from black tea in 1997; we reported in 2011 that they arise from oxidative coupling of pyrogallol-type catechins, but their stereostructures remained undetermined. DFT calculations of the NMR chemical shifts of theacitrin C and TDDFT calculations of the ECD spectrum of its degradation product theacitrinin A established the stereostructures of the theacitrins.

Conference award: Part of this work was presented as a poster by Y. Matsuo at the 8th JSP-CCTCNM-KSP Joint Symposium on Pharmacognosy (Fukuoka, 2014) and received the Best Poster Award.

Matsuo, Y.; Wakamatsu, H.; Omar, M.; Tanaka, T.* Reinvestigation of the Stereochemistry of the C-Glycosidic Ellagitannins, Vescalagin and Castalagin Org. Lett. 2015, 17, 46–49. doi:10.1021/ol503212v

The structures of vescalagin and castalagin, representative C-glycosidic ellagitannins found in wine and other sources, were first reported in the 1960s–70s and, after revisions made by 1990, had been widely accepted. We reinvestigated their stereochemistry computationally. DFT calculations of ¹H and ¹³C NMR chemical shifts and ¹H–¹H coupling constants, together with TDDFT calculations of the ECD spectra of their des-HHDP analogues, led to a revision of the atropisomerism of the triphenoyl moiety in both compounds.

Conference award: Part of this work was presented as a poster by H. Wakamatsu (then a graduate student, Nagasaki University) at the XXVIIth International Conference on Polyphenols & 8th Tannin Conference (Nagoya, 2014) and received a Poster Prize.

Matsuo, Y.; Tadakuma, F.; Shii, T.; Saito, Y.; Tanaka, T.* Selective oxidation of pyrogallol-type catechins with unripe fruit homogenate of Citrus unshiu and structural revision of oolongtheanins Tetrahedron 2015, 71, 2540–2548. doi:10.1016/j.tet.2015.03.016 ｜ NAOSITE

We previously showed that Japanese pear fruit homogenate oxidizes both pyrogallol- and catechol-type tea catechins: oxidative coupling between the two types gives theaflavins, and coupling between pyrogallol-type catechins gives dehydrotheasinensins. In contrast, unripe fruit homogenate of Citrus unshiu selectively oxidized pyrogallol-type catechins to give only dehydrotheasinensins. Analysis of the oxidation products of epigallocatechin, based on spectroscopic and computational data, led to the structural revision of desgalloyl oolongtheanin, a known polyphenol of oolong and black teas, and to a proposed mechanism for oolongtheanin formation.
