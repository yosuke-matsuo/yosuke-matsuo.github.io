Research

Structures and reactions of plant polyphenols

Overview

I study the structures and reactions of plant polyphenols, focusing on tea catechins and on ellagitannins, a class of hydrolysable tannins. The work combines isolation and structure determination, structure determination and revision with density functional theory (DFT) calculations, and studies of biosynthesis and biomimetic reactions.

Some of the tannins studied here have complex, flexible structures and readily undergo oxidation and other transformations, which makes precise structure analysis and elucidation of their transformation processes challenging.

1. Isolation and structure determination of polyphenols

I isolate new polyphenols from plants and determine their structures by NMR and mass spectrometry. For ellagitannins I also capture unstable metabolites by chemical derivatization.

2. Structure determination and revision by computational chemistry

I predict NMR chemical shifts, ¹H–¹H coupling constants and electronic circular dichroism (ECD) spectra with DFT and compare them with experiment to analyze configurations and solution conformations and conformer distributions. For several natural products, I have re-examined previously proposed structures and revised them.

3. Biosynthesis and biomimetic reactions of polyphenols

For catechins, I study the oxidation reactions that occur during black- and oolong-tea processing and the structures of the resulting polyphenols. For ellagitannins, I examine how they are formed and transformed in plants through structural analysis of their constituents and biomimetic reactions. These findings are applied to the biomimetic synthesis of natural products.
